Health Disclaimer

Effective date: 23 October 2026

NutriBuddy is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. It is a general wellness tool for healthy adults. Always talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet, exercise or weight — and never ignore professional advice because of something you saw in the app.

Estimates, not measurements

  • Daily targets (calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, water, steps) are calculated with widely used formulas for healthy adults, such as the Harris–Benedict equation. Real needs vary from person to person.
  • Photo scans and meal descriptions are analysed by artificial intelligence. AI can misidentify foods, miss hidden ingredients such as oil, sugar or sauces, and misjudge portion sizes. Treat the numbers as an approximation and correct them when you know better.
  • Barcode data comes from Open Food Facts, a public database, and can be incomplete or out of date. Check the product label.
  • Projected dates for reaching a target weight assume a steady pace. Weight naturally fluctuates by day; real progress is rarely a straight line.
  • Steps and active energy come from your phone or wearable and are themselves estimates.

The Nutri coach

Nutri is an AI assistant that gives general nutrition and habit guidance. It cannot examine you, does not know your medical history beyond what you tell the app, and can make mistakes. It is instructed to refer clinical questions to a professional, but you should not rely on it for diagnosis, treatment, medication or medical decisions.

Talk to a professional first if you

  • are pregnant, trying to become pregnant or breastfeeding;
  • have, or have had, an eating disorder or a difficult relationship with food;
  • have diabetes, kidney, liver, heart or thyroid disease, or another chronic condition;
  • take medication that affects appetite, blood sugar or weight;
  • are recovering from surgery or illness;
  • plan to lose more than about 1 kg (2 lb) per week, or to eat less than about 1,200 kcal a day (women) or 1,500 kcal a day (men).

Stop and get help

Stop using the app and contact a health professional if tracking food makes you anxious, if you feel unable to stop restricting food, or if you notice symptoms such as dizziness, fainting, palpitations or extreme fatigue. If you are in crisis or in danger, call your local emergency number (112 in Türkiye and the EU, 911 in the US).

Support for eating disorders is available from organisations such as NEDA (US), Beat (UK) and your local health services.

No guarantee of results

Results depend on many factors outside the app's control, including genetics, health conditions, sleep, stress and how consistently a plan is followed. We do not promise any particular weight loss, gain or health outcome.
