Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Applies to the NutriBuddy app on iOS and Android

The short version

  • NutriBuddy has no user accounts and no servers of its own. Your profile, meals, weights, water, steps and chats are stored only on your device.
  • When you use an AI feature (photo scan, describing a meal, barcode lookup fallback, the Nutri coach), the content you submit is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the answer. We ask for your permission before the first time this happens.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, use analytics or tracking SDKs, or track you across apps and websites.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Profile → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the developer is the data controller for the processing described in this policy.

2. Data stored on your device

The following information is created by you and stored locally in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to any server operated by us — we do not operate any.

CategoryExamples
Profile and goalsSex, age, height, current and target weight, activity level, goal and pace, diet style, food restrictions and preferences, motivations and other answers you give during onboarding, calorie/macro/water/step targets

Device backups: if you have enabled iCloud Backup (iOS) or Google device backup (Android), your operating system may include the app's local data in your personal device backup. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple or Google, not by us.

3. Data that leaves your device

3.1 AI features — Google Gemini API

NutriBuddy uses the Gemini API, provided by Google LLC, to analyse food and to power the Nutri coach. Data is sent only when you actively use one of these features, and only after you have given permission in the app:

FeatureWhat is sent
Photo scanThe meal photo you take or choose, and the app language
Describe a meal (text or voice)The text you typed or the transcript of what you said
Barcode scanThe barcode number, and only if the product is not found in Open Food Facts (see 3.2)
Nutri coachYour message and any photo you attach; a short summary of your profile and goals (for example age, sex, height, weight, target weight, diet style, food restrictions and the onboarding answers listed in section 2); a summary of today's log (calories and macros eaten, meal names and times, water and step count); and your most recent messages in the conversation so the coach can follow it

We do not send your name, email address, contact details, precise location or any device identifier to Google — the app does not collect them.

Google processes this data to return the response. Under the Gemini API terms that apply to paid use, Google does not use prompts or responses to improve its products, and may retain them for a limited period to detect abuse and comply with law. See the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Google may process data in the United States and other countries.

AI results are estimates. Please read the Health Disclaimer.

3.2 Barcode lookup — Open Food Facts

When you scan a packaged product, the barcode number is sent to the public Open Food Facts database to retrieve its nutrition information. No other data is sent. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

3.3 Voice input — your phone's speech recognition

If you use the microphone, your speech is converted to text by the speech-recognition service built into your phone (Apple Speech on iOS, Google speech services on Android). Depending on your device and settings, audio may be processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. NutriBuddy does not record or store audio; it only receives the text.

3.4 Apple Health and Health Connect

If you choose to connect, NutriBuddy reads your step count, active energy burned and body weight from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) to keep your daily targets accurate. NutriBuddy does not write data to these services. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or Health Connect settings.

Data obtained from Apple Health or Health Connect is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never used to build user profiles for third parties. Your step count for the current day may be included in the summary sent to the Nutri coach (section 3.1) when you use it.

3.5 Purchases — App Store and Google Play

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or store your payment details. Apple or Google tells the app only whether you have an active subscription.

3.6 Notifications

Meal, water and trial reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification server is involved.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites (the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission because it does not track).
  • No sale or rental of personal data.
  • No use of your data to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

  • On your device: until you delete it in the app, use Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Google (Gemini): for the limited period described in Google's terms (section 3.1).
  • Support emails: for as long as needed to resolve your request, and no longer than 2 years.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — for example the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurumu) or your local EU/UK data protection authority. Residents of California and other US states have similar rights under state law; we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Because your data lives on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app: view and edit it in Profile, and erase it with Profile → Delete all data. For anything else, email ergun.app.dev@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. As we hold no account data on servers, we generally cannot identify you or retrieve data from your device on your behalf.

8. Children

NutriBuddy is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process data of anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, deleting the app removes all of its data.

9. Security

Data is stored in the app's sandboxed storage protected by your phone's operating system and device lock. Data sent to Google and Open Food Facts is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS). Keep your device protected with a passcode; anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app.

10. International transfers

Data you send through AI features is processed by Google, which may store and process it in the United States and other countries. Where required, this transfer relies on your explicit consent and on the safeguards Google provides in its terms.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change materially affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
