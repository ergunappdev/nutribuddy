Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not inside the app.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → NutriBuddy → Cancel Subscription. You can also open it from Profile → Manage subscription in the app.
  • Android: Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → NutriBuddy → Cancel.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date or before your free trial ends to avoid being charged. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple) or through Google Play. We cannot issue store refunds ourselves.

I bought Premium but the app does not show it

Open Profile → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. Make sure you have an internet connection.

How do I delete my data?

NutriBuddy has no accounts and stores your data only on your phone. To delete everything, go to Profile → Delete all data, or uninstall the app. This cannot be undone. There is nothing stored on our side to delete. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Your data is included in your normal device backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup, if enabled) and comes back when you restore that backup to a new phone. There is no separate cloud sync.

The calorie estimate from my photo looks wrong

Photo estimates are approximate. Hidden oil, sauces and portion size are the most common causes of error. You can edit any value before saving, and tips for better results are: photograph from above in good light, keep the whole plate in the frame, and add a short note such as "1 tbsp olive oil" when describing a meal.

Nutri or the photo scan is not responding

AI features need an internet connection. If you are online and it still fails, the AI service may be busy — please try again in a minute. Free users have a limited number of AI uses; Premium removes the limit.

Steps or weight from Apple Health / Health Connect are not showing

Check that NutriBuddy has permission: on iPhone, Health app → Sharing → Apps → NutriBuddy; on Android, Health Connect → App permissions → NutriBuddy. Then pull down on the Home screen to refresh.

Reminders do not arrive

Allow notifications for NutriBuddy in your phone's settings, and check that Focus / Do Not Disturb or battery saver is not blocking them.
