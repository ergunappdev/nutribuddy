NutriBuddy

Calorie and nutrition tracker with an AI coach

NutriBuddy helps you reach your weight goal with a personal daily plan. Snap a photo of your meal or describe it in a sentence and the app estimates its calories and macronutrients. Track water, steps and weight, see your progress in clear charts, and ask Nutri — your friendly raccoon coach — what to eat next.

Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your logs stay on your phone. Only what you choose to analyse with AI is sent to Google's Gemini API, with your permission.
